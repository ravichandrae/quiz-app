# Schoolmela Quiz — Student guide

This guide is for students aged 12–15. Teachers can read it aloud or print it.
It works the same on the website and in the Android app.

## 1. Make your account (once)

1. Tap **Create an account** (on the app: **New here? Create an account**).
2. Type **your name** and **your mobile number** (10 numbers).
3. **Choose a PIN**: 4 to 6 numbers. Type it again to check.
   - Remember your PIN. Do not tell it to friends.
4. School name and email are optional.
5. Tap **Create account**. You are now logged in.

## 2. Log in

Type your **mobile number** and **PIN**, then tap **Log in**.

- "Wrong mobile number or PIN" — check the numbers and try again.
- After **5 wrong PINs** you must wait **15 minutes**.
- **Forgot your PIN?** Ask your teacher. They can give you a new one.
- On a shared school computer or tablet, tap **Log out** when you finish.

## 3. Your quizzes

**My quizzes** shows the quizzes your teacher gave you.

- **New** — you have not started it. Tap **Start**.
- **Continue** — you started it but did not finish.
- **Done** — you finished it. You see your score. Tap **See results**.
- **Finish by** — try to finish before this day.

## 4. Taking a quiz

1. Read the rules and tap **Start quiz**.
2. Each question has its **own timer**. The bar goes down; it turns **red** in the last 10 seconds.
3. Tap your answer (A, B, C or D), then tap **Submit**.
   - Only answers you **submit** before the time runs out count.
   - If time runs out, the next question comes by itself.
4. You **cannot go back** to a question.
5. If the internet stops, your answer stays chosen. Tap **Try again**.
6. If you leave the quiz, **the timer keeps running**. Come back soon and tap **Continue**.

## 5. Your score

At the end you see your score, for example **8 / 10 (80%)**.

- Tap **See your results** to see each question:
  ✓ **Right**, ✗ **Wrong**, ⏱ **Time ran out**, or **Not reached**.
- Some teachers show the correct answers later in class instead.
- **My scores** keeps all your finished quizzes.

You can take each quiz **once**.
