# Schoolmela Quiz — Teacher (admin) guide

Teachers use the **website**. The Android app is for students only.

Log in with your mobile number and PIN. The first admin account is set up when the system is
installed; after that, any admin can add others (see *Students → Add another admin*).

The menu at the top has **Students, Groups, Questions, Quizzes, Results**.

## Students

Students register themselves. The **Students** page lists them, newest first.

- **Search** by name or mobile number, and filter by *Active* / *Turned off*.
- **Locked** means the student typed a wrong PIN 5 times. It clears by itself after 15 minutes.
- **Reset PIN** — when a student forgets their PIN. Choose the new PIN **with** the student, type
  it twice and tap **Save new PIN**. Their old PIN stops working, any lock is removed and they are
  logged out everywhere.
- **Turn off** — the student can no longer log in, and is logged out at once. **Turn on** undoes it.
- **Add another admin** (bottom of the page) — another teacher's name, mobile number and PIN.

## Groups

Groups, such as a class, let you give a quiz to many students at once.

1. **Groups → New group name → Create group**.
2. On the group's page, search for students and tap **Add**. **Remove** takes a student out.

Students who join a group later also get the group's quizzes. A student who is removed no longer
sees them (their finished results are kept).

## Questions

**Questions → Add question**:

1. Type the question (up to 500 characters).
2. Fill in **all four answers** (they must be different) and choose the **correct one**.
3. Choose the **time to answer** (30 seconds to 2 minutes).
4. **Save question**.

Questions are kept in the **question bank** and can be used in many quizzes. **Edit** changes a
question; quizzes students have already started keep the old version. A question used in a
quiz cannot be deleted until you remove it from that quiz.

## Quizzes

**Quizzes → Create quiz**:

1. Give it a **title**.
2. Optional: **Set a time limit for the whole quiz** (in minutes). If the questions' own times add
   up to more, you will see a warning: slow students may not reach the last questions.
3. **Show students the correct answers after they finish** — untick it if you prefer to go
   through the answers in class.
4. Under **Question bank**, search and tap **Add**. Use **↑ ↓** to change the order and
   **Remove** to take one out.
5. **Save quiz**.

## Giving a quiz to students

On **Quizzes**, tap **Assign** on the quiz.

- Choose **A group**, **One student**, or **All students** (this includes students who register
  later).
- Optional: **Finish by** a date. Students can still start after it; their result is marked
  **Late**.
- Tap **Give quiz**. *Who has this quiz* lists everyone it is given to; **Remove** takes it away.

Students see new quizzes marked **New** when they next open the app or website.

## Results

**Results** (or **Results** on a quiz's row) lists every attempt, newest first.

- Filter by **quiz**, and search by student name or mobile number.
- **Not finished** — the student started but has not finished. **Late** — started after the due date.
- **View** shows each question: the student's answer, the correct answer, and the time taken.
- **Download CSV** saves the results you are looking at as a spreadsheet file (name, mobile,
  school, quiz, score, percentage, start and finish times, late).

## Good to know

- Each student can take each quiz **once**.
- Timing is decided by the server, so changing the clock on a phone does not help anyone.
- If a student closes the quiz, the current question's timer keeps running. They can continue
  from the next question.
